Our Story

From a shared spreadsheet to an adaptive protocol.

2022

The problem, up close

Co-founder and strength coach Hassan Warraich benched a client two weeks before a competition — the client's HRV had been dropping for ten straight days and no app had said a word.

2023

Vitalis is founded

Hassan teamed up with sports scientist Dr. Ayesha Farooq and engineer Omar Sheikh to turn the manual readiness checks he'd been doing by hand into software that could do it for every member, every morning.

2024

First 1,000 members

Word spread through Karachi's strength and running communities. The waitlist for 1:1 coaching hit three months before Vitalis hired its first outside coach.

2025

Weekly coach review, at scale

Every plan is still checked by a human weekly. Growth meant hiring more certified coaches, not removing them from the loop.

2026

Members in 18 countries

Vitalis now supports remote athletes, desk-bound professionals rebuilding a training habit, and competitive lifters — all on the same adaptive core.

What We Believe

Three rules that shape every feature we ship.

Rule 01

Recovery is data, not an excuse

We treat sleep, HRV, and stress as inputs to the plan — not as reasons to skip it. If your body needs a lighter day, the plan says so before you have to ask.

Rule 02

A human checks the machine

The model proposes; a certified coach reviews. No member's program runs a full month without a real person looking at the trend lines.

Rule 03

Adherence beats intensity

A hard plan you quit in week three loses to a sustainable one you're still running in month six. We optimize for the second.
