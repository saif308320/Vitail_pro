FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from your account settings — changes apply from your next billing cycle.

Which wearables does Vitalis support?

Vitalis syncs with all major wearables including Whoop, Oura, Apple Watch, and Garmin. HRV and sleep data update automatically each morning.

Do I need gym access?

No. During onboarding you tell us what equipment you have — full gym, home setup, or bodyweight only — and every protocol adapts to it.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, all plans are month-to-month with no lock-in contract. Cancel from your account settings in under a minute.

What happens in the free assessment?

A 12-minute intake covering training history, current recovery markers, and goals, used to generate your first week's protocol before you pay anything.
