Vitalis Performance Lab ("Vitalis," "we," "us") provides adaptive training, recovery, and nutrition coaching. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, app, and coaching services, and how we use, share, and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information collected automatically, and information from connected third-party services:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, password.
  • Profile information: age range, training history, goals, equipment access, injury history you choose to share.
  • Billing information: processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, device and browser type, approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Communications: messages you send through the contact form, email, or in-app chat with a coach.

2. Biometric & Wearable Data

When you connect a supported wearable (such as Whoop, Oura, Apple Watch, or Garmin), we receive biometric signals including heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, sleep stages and duration, and workout activity. This data is used solely to calculate your daily readiness score and generate your adaptive protocol.

You can disconnect a wearable at any time from your account settings. Disconnecting stops new data from syncing but does not automatically delete data already collected — see Section 6 for how to request deletion.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate and adjust your daily training, recovery, and nutrition protocol.
  • To enable your assigned coach to review your trends and communicate with you.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Vitalis app and website.
  • To send service updates, billing notices, and — if you opt in — product news and coaching tips.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal or biometric data. We share information only with:

  • Your assigned coach and the Vitalis coaching team, to deliver the service.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf (hosting, payments, customer support, analytics), under contracts that limit their use of your data.
  • Authorities, where required to comply with a legal obligation or protect the rights and safety of Vitalis or its members.
  • A successor entity, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and biometric data for as long as your account is active, and for up to 24 months after cancellation to support reactivation and to meet legal and accounting obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent for wearable data syncing at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@vitalisperformance.com — we respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls limiting who on our team can view raw biometric data, and regular security reviews. No system is completely secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

8. Children's Privacy

Vitalis is intended for users aged 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services evolve. Material changes will be notified by email or an in-app notice at least 14 days before taking effect. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to privacy@vitalisperformance.com or mailed to Vitalis Performance Lab, 4th Floor, Ocean Towers, Block 9, Clifton, Karachi, Pakistan.
